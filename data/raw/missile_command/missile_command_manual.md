# MISSILE COMMAND - Game Manual

## Game Overview
Missile Command is a defense-themed arcade game by Atari released in 1980. Defend six cities from incoming ballistic missiles using three anti-ballistic missile (ABM) batteries.

## Controls
- **Trackball/Mouse**: Position crosshair cursor on screen
- **LEFT Button**: Fire from left battery (Alpha)
- **CENTER Button**: Fire from center battery (Delta)  
- **RIGHT Button**: Fire from right battery (Omega)

## Gameplay

### Objective
Defend your cities and missile batteries from enemy missiles, planes, and satellites. Survive as many waves as possible to achieve the highest score.

### Cities
- Six cities positioned across bottom of screen
- Each city worth 100 points if surviving at wave end
- Destroyed cities remain destroyed (never rebuild)
- Game ends when all cities are destroyed

### Missile Batteries
Three ABM batteries with 10 missiles each:
- **Alpha Base** (left): Defends left side
- **Delta Base** (center): Defends middle
- **Omega Base** (right): Defends right side

Battery placement:
- Alpha: Far left
- Delta: Center (between city groups)
- Omega: Far right

### Battery Mechanics
- Start each wave with 10 missiles per battery
- Batteries can be destroyed by enemy fire
- Destroyed batteries are rebuilt between waves
- Missiles reload at wave end
- Bonus missiles awarded for surviving cities

## Enemy Threats

### Ballistic Missiles
- Drop vertically from top of screen
- Travel in straight lines to targets
- Split into MIRVs (Multiple Independently-targetable Reentry Vehicles)
- Increasing numbers in later waves

### Bombers
- Fly horizontally across screen
- Drop missiles while moving
- Worth 100 points when destroyed
- Appear in later waves

### Satellites
- Move across top of screen
- Split into multiple warheads
- Worth 100 points when destroyed
- Introduced in advanced waves

### Smart Bombs
- Evade explosions actively
- More difficult to destroy
- Appear in higher waves

## ABM System
Your missiles:
- Launch from selected battery
- Explode at crosshair position
- Create expanding explosion radius
- Destroy enemy missiles within blast
- Chain reactions possible
- Missiles limited - choose shots carefully

## Scoring System
- Enemy missile destroyed: 25 points
- Bomber destroyed: 100 points
- Satellite destroyed: 100 points  
- Surviving city (end of wave): 100 points
- Surviving missile: 5 points
- Bonus cities awarded at 10,000, 20,000, 30,000 points

## Wave Progression
Waves increase in difficulty:
- Wave 1: Few slow missiles
- Wave 2+: More missiles, faster speeds
- Wave 4+: Bombers introduced
- Wave 6+: Satellites added
- Wave 8+: Smart bombs appear
- Wave 10+: Multiple MIRVs, overwhelming numbers

### Bonus Scoring
At wave end:
- 100 points per surviving city
- 5 points per unused missile
- Bonus missiles added (up to 10 per battery)

## Strategy Tips

1. **Prioritize Targets**
   - Stop missiles targeting cities first
   - Batteries are less critical (they rebuild)
   - Focus on nearest threats

2. **Explosion Management**
   - Use explosion radius efficiently
   - Create chain reactions
   - Intercept multiple missiles with one shot

3. **Battery Selection**
   - Use nearest battery to target
   - Conserve center battery - covers most area
   - Don't deplete one battery completely

4. **Crosshair Placement**
   - Lead fast-moving targets
   - Place explosions in missile paths
   - Don't wait for missiles to reach cities

5. **MIRV Handling**
   - Destroy parent missile before it splits
   - If split, use wide explosion to catch multiples

6. **Smart Bomb Defense**
   - Predict evasion patterns
   - Use multiple overlapping explosions
   - Don't waste too many missiles

7. **Endgame Management**
   - Protect at least one city
   - Accept battery losses to save cities
   - Focus defense on one side if necessary

## Advanced Techniques

### Defensive Zones
Create persistent explosion fields in high-traffic areas to destroy multiple incoming missiles.

### Battery Load Balancing
Distribute shots across batteries to maintain coverage if one is destroyed.

### Sacrifice Strategy
In overwhelming situations, focus defense on 1-2 cities rather than losing all six.

## Technical Information
- Developer: Atari, Inc.
- Designer: Dave Theurer
- Release Date: July 1980
- Display: Raster graphics
- Theme: Inspired by Cold War nuclear tensions
- Legacy: Popularized trackball controls

## Trivia
The game cannot be won - waves continue indefinitely with increasing difficulty until all cities are destroyed. The designer intended this to reflect the futility of nuclear war.
